Select and change text in Visual mode (Modeless off)

Put the cursor on "quick" below. Press v, then w.
The badge says VISUAL and the selection is orange.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.

Press y to copy it. The cursor goes back to its start.
Press $ then p to paste it at the end of the line.

Put the cursor inside the parentheses. Press v, then i(.

call(first, second, third)

Press c to delete the selection and type something new.
Press Esc when done.

Selecting with Shift and the arrows, Ctrl+A, or the mouse
also switches to VISUAL. Esc goes back to NORMAL.
