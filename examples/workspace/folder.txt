Press Shift+Space and type workspace.
Enter with no path lists folders you have opened before.
Up and Down move in that list. Enter opens the highlighted folder.
Type a path instead, such as .. or a full path. Right accepts the dim hint.
Yes saves unsaved files first. No leaves them unsaved. Cancel stays here.
The folder you leave is remembered, and its tabs come back when you return.
Files from the folder you left close. A relative save writes in the folder you opened.
